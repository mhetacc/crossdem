Speech Map

Each speech is represented by the TF-IDF-weighted average of the fastText vectors of its words, so distinctive words count more than common ones. The vectors are projected once with t-SNE (2D and 3D), so dots keep their position as you change the selection. Nearby dots are speeches with similar vocabulary.

Group speeches by Prime Minister, by Year Period (1945–1950, then five-year buckets), or by Political Leaning. Max docs/group randomly subsamples large groups (with a fixed seed) to keep the plot readable.
